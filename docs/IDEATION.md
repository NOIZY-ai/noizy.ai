# NOIZY Ideation Compendium

> One place to relearn the ideas behind NOIZY. Each section summarizes the idea and points to the source document in this repo.
> Spelling rule: the brand is always **NOIZY** (uppercase, with a Z).

---

## 1. The Core Belief

**Creators own their voice, persona and work, always.** NOIZY replaces extraction with infrastructure: consent, provenance and fair pay are enforced in code, not in policy.

- Tagline: **TOGETHER, WE RISE.** Rallying cry: **GORUNFREE.**
- Sources: `MANIFESTO.md`, `heaven/gospel/PUBLIC-MANIFESTO.md`, `heaven/gospel/CATHEDRAL.md`, `heaven/gospel/SHORT-FORM.md`

## 2. The Law (non-negotiables)

| Idea | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Sovereignty** | Voice, persona and life stay with the creator. Consent is required, revocation is unconditional, and a kill switch is always on. |
| **Plowman Standard (75/25)** | Creator keeps 75%, in perpetuity. It is built into the architecture and cannot be renegotiated. |
| **Voice Estate / legacy** | Credits and tokens can be assigned to family, or committed to the Griot Fund for future creators. |
| **Seven Permanent Laws** | Creation before optimization, Consent before generation, Provenance before scale, Memory before motion, Verification before deployment, Sovereignty before convenience, No output without lineage. |

Sources: `MANIFESTO.md`, `IMPERIAL_CHARTER.md`, `heaven/gospel/COVENANT.md`

## 3. The Problem: "The DREED"

"Dreadful Greed": an industry that harvests voices and music as training data without consent, credit or royalties. The briefing documents real cases (voice-cloning and music-generation lawsuits) and states what NOIZY would have done differently in each.

Sources: `heaven/gospel/THE-DREED-BRIEFING.md`, `heaven/gospel/SIX-SYSTEM-FAILURES.md`

## 5. NOIZYVOX Positioning

- Not a TTS tool or a marketplace. It is a **curated agency** that licenses performance, never ownership.
- **Artist-centric discovery:** match on creative identity (statement, philosophy, emotional territories, lineage, boundaries), not demographics or price. "A vending machine vs. a collaborative studio."
- **Premium** means signature sound, artist control and per-project approvals, transparent perpetual royalties, and trust built into the infrastructure.
- Sources: `heaven/noizyvox/AGENCY-POSITIONING.md`, `ARTIST-CENTRIC-DISCOVERY.md`, `MARKET-INTELLIGENCE.md`, `MULTILINGUAL-CHARACTER-SPEC.md`
