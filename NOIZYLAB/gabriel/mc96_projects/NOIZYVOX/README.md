# 🎙️ NOIZYVOX - Voice Actor Platform
**Project Concept:** Voice Actor Licensing & Revenue Platform

---

## 📋 PROJECT OVERVIEW

NoizyVox is a platform that allows professional voice actors to:
- Own their AI voices
- License voices to clients
- Share revenue (70% to actor)
- Manage voice training
- Track usage and payments
