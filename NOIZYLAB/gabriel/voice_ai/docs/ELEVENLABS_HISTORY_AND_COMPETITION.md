## 🏛️ THE PIONEERS (BEFORE ELEVENLABS)

### **1. CereProc** (2005) - THE OLDEST PIONEER
- **Founded:** 2005 (17 years before ElevenLabs!)
- **Location:** Edinburgh, Scotland
- **Specialty:** Natural & expressive TTS voices
- **Features:**
  - Regional accents
  - Voice cloning capabilities
  - Used by people with motor neuron disease
- **Status:** ✅ Still active
- **Note:** THE ORIGINAL VOICE CLONING PIONEER

### **2. Google DeepMind WaveNet** (2016)
- **Launched:** 2016 (6 years before ElevenLabs)
- **Impact:** Set new standards for natural speech
- **Technology:** Deep neural network for raw audio waveforms
- **Integration:** Google Assistant, Cloud TTS
- **Status:** ✅ Still active (Google Cloud TTS)

### **3. Amazon Polly** (2016)
- **Launched:** 2016
- **Specialty:** Cloud-based text-to-speech
- **Features:**
  - Multiple languages
  - Various voices
  - Widely adopted
- **Status:** ✅ Still active (AWS)

### **4. Microsoft Azure Speech** (Pre-2022)
- **Launched:** Before 2022
- **Specialty:** Enterprise speech services
- **Features:**

- **Status:** ✅ Still active (we have this set up!)

### **5. IBM Watson Text to Speech** (Pre-2022)
- **Launched:** Before 2022

- **Status:** ✅ Still active

### **6. Respeecher** (2018) - FILM/TV PIONEER
