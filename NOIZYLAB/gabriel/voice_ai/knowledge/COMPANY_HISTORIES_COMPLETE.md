## 🍎 APPLE INC. (1976-Present)

### **Founding:**
- **April 1, 1976** - Founded by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, Ronald Wayne
- **Location:** Cupertino, California
- **First Product:** Apple I ($666.66) - motherboard with CPU, RAM, basic video chips

### **Key Milestones:**
- **1977:** Apple II - First successful mass-produced personal computer
- **1984:** Macintosh - First mass-market GUI computer with mouse
- **1997:** Steve Jobs returns, company revitalized
- **1998:** iMac G3 - Translucent design revolution
- **2001:** iPod - Revolutionized portable music
- **2001:** First Apple Stores open
- **2003:** iTunes Store launches
- **2007:** iPhone - Transformed mobile industry
- **2010:** iPad - Created tablet market
- **2015:** Apple Watch - Wearable tech leader
- **2015:** Apple Music - Streaming service
- **2019:** Apple TV+ - Original content
- **2020:** Apple Silicon (M1) - Custom chips
- **2025:** Market cap $3+ trillion

### **Product Evolution:**
- **Computers:** Apple I → Apple II → Lisa → Macintosh → iMac → MacBook → Mac Studio
- **Mobile:** iPhone → iPad → Apple Watch
- **Services:** iTunes → App Store → iCloud → Apple Music → Apple TV+
- **Chips:** PowerPC → Intel → Apple Silicon (M-series)
