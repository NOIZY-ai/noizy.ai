# ALLIANCE INTELLIGENCE: THE WIDER AI LAB
**Scope**: OpenAI | Meta | Anthropic | Stability | Suno
**Objective**: TOTAL AWARENESS

## 1. OPENAI (The Sora Engine)
*   **Sora (Video)**:
    *   **Architecture**: Diffusion Transformer (DiT). Treats video patches as tokens (like GPT-4 treats words).
    *   **Power**: Generates up to 1 minute of 1080p video with complex camera motion and character consistency.
    *   **Simulation**: Effectively learns a "World Model" (physics, object permanence).
*   **Voice Engine**:
    *   **Capability**: 15-second clone sample. High-fidelity text-to-speech with emotive cloning.
*   **GPT-4o (Omni)**:
    *   **Native Audio**: Can sing, whisper, and emote in real-time with <300ms latency.

## 2. META (The Open Source King)
*   **AudioCraft (MusicGen & AudioGen)**:
    *   ** Status**: **OPEN SOURCE & LOCALLY RUNNABLE.**
    *   **MusicGen**: Generates music from text ("lo-fi hip hop").
    *   **AudioGen**: Generates sound effects ("dog barking in a hallway").
    *   **EnCodec**: High-fidelity neural audio compression.
*   **Emu Video**:
    *   **Technique**: Factorized video generation (Image -> Video).

## 3. ANTHROPIC (The Reasoning Eye)
*   **Claude 3.5 Sonnet**:
    *   **Vision**: While not generating video, it has SOTA (State of the Art) video *analysis*.
    *   **Usage**: Feed it frame-by-frame screenshots to extract complex narrative and code from video UIs.

## 4. STABILITY AI & SUNO
*   **Stable Audio 2.0**:
    *   **Structure**: Latent Diffusion.
    *   **Feature**: Generates full tracks (up to 3 minutes) with structure (Intro, Verse, Chorus).
*   **Suno v3/v4**:
    *   **Specialty**: Full song generation *with lyrics and vocals*.
    *   **Architecture**: Autoregressive Transformers specialized for high-fidelity vocal synthesis.
