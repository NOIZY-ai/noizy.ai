Summarize this content. Provide:

## HOW TO ADD

1. Open **Settings** on iPhone/iPad
2. Tap **General**
3. Tap **Keyboard**
4. Tap **Text Replacement**
5. Tap **+** in top right
6. **Phrase**: The full code (e.g., `/SS-SYS-SUM`)
7. **Shortcut**: Your abbreviation (e.g., `sssum`)
8. Tap **Save**

---

## PRO TIPS

1. **Keep shortcuts consistent**: Always start with `ss`
2. **Use logical abbreviations**: `sscode` for code, `sssum` for summary
3. **Test immediately**: Type in Notes app to verify
4. **Sync across devices**: iCloud syncs Text Replacements automatically
5. **Backup**: Screenshot your replacements list
